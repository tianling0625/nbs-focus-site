Privacy Policy

NBS MD Reader is a read-only Markdown reader for iPhone and iPad, developed by Tianyu Wang.

Data collected by the app

NBS MD Reader does not collect or transmit personal information or document contents to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Documents and local storage

The app accesses documents you select through the system file picker or open with the app. Markdown is processed on your device. Your original documents are not edited.

The app stores reading preferences, recent document names and file-access bookmarks, and saved reading positions locally on your device. These are used to restore your reading experience and are not sent to the developer. Device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple settings.

iCloud Drive and external services

When you select a document from iCloud Drive or another file provider, the provider manages its storage and synchronization under its own privacy policy. NBS MD Reader does not operate a document-storage server.

If you open an external link from a document, the destination website or app may collect information under its own policy. Copying a code block places the selected text on the system clipboard.

Support email

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive documents. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users, including children. It does not include social networking, messaging, or advertising features.

Your choices

You can remove recent entries from the app without deleting the original documents. Deleting the app removes its local settings and history, subject to any device backup restored later. Manage iCloud Drive documents and backups through Apple's settings.

This website

This support website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or application cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. This is separate from the iOS app's data practices.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
