Quick start

From file to reading.

  1. Save a UTF-8 .md or .markdown file in Files or iCloud Drive.
  2. Open NBS MD Reader and tap Open Markdown, then choose your file.
  3. Use the outline to jump to headings, search for passages, and open AA to change the theme, font size, spacing, or content width.

iCloud Drive may need to download a file before it can be opened. Recently opened files must remain available at their original location.

Common questions

A few useful answers.

Does the app edit my files?

No. NBS MD Reader is read-only. It reads selected documents without changing the original file.

What if a recent file will not open?

The file may have moved, been deleted, or become unavailable through its file provider. Tap Open Markdown and select it again from Files.

Are Mermaid diagrams and LaTeX formulas rendered?

Version 1.0 displays Mermaid and LaTeX code blocks as source text. Diagram and formula rendering are not currently supported.

Are my documents uploaded?

NBS MD Reader processes document contents on your device and does not upload them to the developer. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that service handles storage according to its own settings and policies.

How do I remove reading history?

Touch and hold a recent document and choose Remove. This removes its recent-list entry without deleting the original document. Removing the app clears its local settings and history, subject to any device backup restored later.

Your files stay yours.

No account, ads, or analytics SDKs in the app. Reading settings, recent files, and reading positions are stored locally.
